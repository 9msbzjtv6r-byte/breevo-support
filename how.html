How Breevo works

No black box.

What Breevo does with your documents, where AI fits in, and how you can check for yourself that nothing leaves your iPhone.

What does Breevo do, exactly?

  1. Reads your documents. You add your W-2s, 1099s, 1098s, pay stubs or last year's return. Breevo finds the numbers on them, such as your wages and the tax already taken out.
  2. Works out your return. It applies the federal and state tax rules for that year, line by line, the same way the IRS forms do.
  3. Shows you everything. Your Return lists every line with how it was worked out. If Breevo read a number wrong, you can change it.
  4. Finds ways to pay less. It shows legal options, such as a 401(k), IRA or HSA, with what each could save. You decide what to do.
  5. Gets you ready to file. It fills in the official IRS forms for you to print and mail, makes one PDF for your CPA, or shows what to type to file online yourself.

Does Breevo use AI?

The math is not AI. Every number comes from fixed tax rules written from the IRS's and states' own forms and instructions. The same documents always give the same answer, and Breevo shows how it got each one.

Reading uses Apple's tools on your iPhone. Apple's text recognition reads your documents on the phone itself. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device model also helps read pages that don't follow a standard form, like a handwritten note. It runs on your iPhone, not in the cloud, and Breevo marks anything it read that way for you to check.

How is my data kept safe?

  • It stays on your iPhone. No account, no sign-in, no servers. Nothing is sent anywhere, unless you share it.
  • It's locked with strong encryption. Only your iPhone has the key.
  • Breevo locks when you leave it for a while. Face ID or your passcode opens it again.
  • It's kept out of iCloud and computer backups. On a new iPhone, add your documents again.
  • It's never sold or shared. Apple's privacy label for Breevo says Data Not Collected.
  • You can erase it any time: Erase all Breevo data, at the bottom of About and Privacy, or delete the app.

How can I be sure nothing leaves my phone?

Breevo is built without any code that connects to the internet, and every version is checked for it before release. You don't have to take that on trust. Two ways to check:

Airplane Mode

Turn on Airplane Mode. Breevo works exactly the same, because it never needs a connection.

App Privacy Report

Open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then App Privacy Report, and turn it on. It lists every server each app contacts. Breevo contacts none.

The only time anything leaves your iPhone is when you share it yourself, such as sending the PDF to your CPA with the Share button.

How does Breevo keep up with tax changes?

Breevo has the tax rules for 2025 and 2026. When the IRS and the states publish new forms and numbers, Breevo gets them through an App Store update, because it never connects to the internet. Keep automatic updates turned on.

Some 2026 amounts are estimates until the IRS or your state publishes final numbers, and Breevo marks them.

To check its math, Breevo runs the IRS's own test returns, the sample returns the IRS publishes for tax software makers. See every result.

Is Breevo giving me tax advice?

Breevo does the math, finds ways to pay less and shows you why. You decide. Check the numbers before you file.

Breevo is a do-it-yourself tax preparation and planning app. It applies published tax rules to your documents, shows the rule behind every number, and gets your return ready for you to review, sign and file.

Breevo doesn't give tax advice. For advice about your own situation, talk to a CPA or tax professional. The PDF for your CPA makes that easy. The Terms of Use say this in full.
