How Breevo works

No black box.

What Breevo does with your documents, where AI fits in, and how you can check for yourself that nothing leaves your iPhone.

What does Breevo do, exactly?

  1. Reads your documents. You add your W-2s, 1099s, 1098s, pay stubs or last year's return. Breevo finds the numbers on them, such as your wages and the tax already taken out.
  2. Works out your return. It applies the federal and state tax rules for that year, line by line, the same way the IRS forms do.
  3. Shows you everything. Your Return lists every line with how it was worked out. If Breevo read a number wrong, you can change it.
  4. Finds ways to pay less. It shows legal options, such as a 401(k), IRA or HSA, with what each could save. You decide what to do.
  5. Gets you ready to file. It fills in the official IRS forms for you to print and mail, makes one PDF for your CPA, or shows what to type to file online yourself.

Does Breevo use AI?

The math is not AI. Every number on your return comes from fixed tax rules written from the IRS's and states' own forms and instructions. The same documents always give the same answer, and Breevo shows how it got each one. Nothing is guessed.

Reading uses Apple's tools on your iPhone. Apple's built-in text recognition reads your documents on the phone itself. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device model also helps read pages that don't follow a standard form, like a handwritten note or a donation letter. It runs on your iPhone, not in the cloud, and Breevo marks anything it read that way for you to check.

How is my data kept safe?

  • It stays on your iPhone. Breevo has no account, no sign-in and no servers. There is nowhere for your data to go.
  • It's encrypted and locked. Your documents and returns are encrypted on the phone and open only with Face ID or your passcode.
  • It's kept out of backups. Breevo's data isn't copied to iCloud or to computer backups.
  • You can erase it any time. Delete one return, erase everything from Breevo's About and Privacy screen, or delete the app.
  • It's never sold or shared. No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Apple's privacy label for Breevo says Data Not Collected.

How can I be sure nothing leaves my phone?

Breevo is built without any code that connects to the internet, and every version is checked for it before release. You don't have to take that on trust. Two ways to check:

Turn on Airplane Mode

Add a document and see your refund. Breevo works exactly the same with no connection, because it never needs one.

Look at the App Privacy Report

On your iPhone, open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then App Privacy Report, and turn it on. It lists every website and server each app contacts. Breevo contacts none, so it has nothing to list.

The only time anything leaves your iPhone is when you share it yourself, such as sending the PDF to your CPA with the Share button.

How does Breevo keep up with tax changes?

Breevo has the rules for each tax year it covers, now 2025 and 2026. Each year, when the IRS and the states publish their final forms and numbers, Breevo is updated through the App Store. Because Breevo never connects to the internet, those App Store updates are how new rules arrive, so keep automatic updates turned on.

To check its math, Breevo runs the IRS's own test returns, the sample returns the IRS publishes for tax software makers. See every result.

Is Breevo giving me tax advice?

No. Breevo is a do-it-yourself tool. It shows the math and ideas that may lower your tax, with what each one depends on. You decide what to do, you review your return, and you sign and file it yourself. For advice about your own situation, talk to a CPA or an enrolled agent; Breevo makes a PDF to bring them.
