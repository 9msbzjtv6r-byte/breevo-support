Privacy Policy

Everything stays on your iPhone

Effective November 6, 2026

In one sentence: Breevo keeps everything on your iPhone. Breevo doesn't collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your information, and can't see it.

What Breevo stores

The documents you add (photos or PDFs) and what Breevo reads from them – names, Social Security numbers, income, taxes and the returns it works out – are stored only on your iPhone, encrypted, and unlocked with Face ID or your device passcode. If your iPhone has no passcode, Breevo still encrypts your data but can't lock it, and reminds you to set one.

What Breevo sends anywhere

Nothing. Breevo has no account, no sign-in and no servers, and it has no way to send your data anywhere. Your documents are read on the iPhone by Apple's built-in tools (and Apple Intelligence where available). There is no advertising, analytics or tracking, and no third-party code that collects data. The only time anything leaves your iPhone is when you choose to share it - for example the CPA package or the filled forms, sent with the iPhone's Share button to the person or app you pick. Breevo never sees where it goes.

Backups

Breevo's data is left out of iCloud and computer backups, and the key that unlocks it never leaves your iPhone. It can't be restored to another phone – keep your original documents, and add them again on a new iPhone.

Deleting your data

  • Delete one return: touch and hold it in the list, then "Delete This Return".
  • Delete everything: in Breevo, tap ••• (More), then "About and Privacy", then "Erase all Breevo data" - or delete the app.

Children

Breevo is a tax tool for adults and isn't directed at children. It collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Support

Questions or problems: support@breevo.si. Please don't send tax documents or Social Security numbers by email.

Breevo is a do-it-yourself tax preparation and planning app. It shows the rule behind each number so you can check it; you review your return and file it yourself.
